# Core palette (approved 11 Nov 2026)

The five colours per mode and the named colours are from the brief (design document, 11 Nov 2026).
Everything below was proposed and approved. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the surface named in the last
column (selection: the selection text on the selection colour).

| Role | Dark | ratio | on | Light | ratio | on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Line (border) | `#484E5A` | 1.88 | window | `#C9CDD4` | 1.45 | window |
| Line, strong | `#697386` | 3.29 | window | `#A9AEB8` | 2.02 | window |
| Muted text | `#C9CDD4` | 9.85 | window | `#5C6578` | 5.32 | window |
| Disabled text | `#697386` | 3.29 | window | `#A4A9B3` | 2.14 | window |
| Selection | `#3F72C6` | 4.74 | #FFFFFF text | `#3F72C6` | 4.74 | #FFFFFF text |
| Link | `#9DC8F5` | 9.65 | view | `#2D5DAF` | 6.37 | view |
| Visited link | `#A99AD8` | 6.67 | view | `#6A58A8` | 5.88 | view |
| Negative (error) | `#F0908F` | 6.80 | window | `#B93A3A` | 5.12 | window |
| Neutral (warning) | `#E8C37E` | 9.38 | window | `#8A6100` | 5.04 | window |
| Positive (success) | `#8FD1A6` | 8.86 | window | `#2A744A` | 5.17 | window |
